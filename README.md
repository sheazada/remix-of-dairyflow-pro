# Remix of DairyFlow Pro

Build a Professional Dairy Distribution ERP Web Application



I want you to build a complete, production-ready web application for my dairy distribution business. This is NOT a simple website. It should look and work like a modern ERP used by large FMCG distributors.



Business Overview



I am a dairy products distributor. I purchase products from dairy companies and supply them to hundreds of retail shops.



I do NOT manufacture products.



My daily workflow is:



- Receive orders from retailers.

- Manage product inventory.

- Prepare delivery routes.

- Generate invoices.

- Track payments.

- Maintain customer balances.

- Analyze sales and profits.

- Generate reports.

- Store all historical business data permanently.



The application should feel as professional as SAP, Zoho Inventory, Oracle NetSuite, Tally Prime, and modern SaaS ERP systems while remaining extremely simple and fast to use.
